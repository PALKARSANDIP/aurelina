# Aurelian prompt pack (version 2)

Fictional marque. No real brands, logos, badges, mascots or monograms. Generated by `tools/build_prompts.py`; edit that file, not this one.

Version 2 redesigns the car so image tools stop drifting to a Rolls-Royce Phantom (see `images/reference/README.txt`).

## Step 1: Car bible

> The Aurelian Halcyon, an original grand saloon design that resembles no existing car brand: a long, low four-door fastback with a very long smooth bonnet, a roofline that slopes in one unbroken curve into a short tapered tail, and flush hidden door handles. Single-colour deep bottle-green metallic paint, mirror gloss, with one fine gold coachline along the shoulder. Low, wide horizontal grille of fine satin-bronze bars, wider than it is tall, linked to the headlamps by one thin full-width light line. Bronze closed-disc wheels with fine radial fluting. Unbadged: plain grille, plain wheel centres, nothing on the bonnet. Interior: cognac-tan leather, dark walnut, brushed bronze.

Short version for video prompts: *the same bottle-green Aurelian Halcyon fastback saloon with its low horizontal bronze grille, thin light-line headlamps, gold coachline and bronze disc wheels, unchanged in every frame*

## Step 2: Style bible

> Photorealistic high-end automotive campaign photography, full-frame cinema camera. 35mm lens for wide and environmental shots, 85mm for three-quarters and details, camera low near headlamp height unless stated. Studio shots: dark charcoal studio with one large rectangular light panel overhead, long soft gradient reflections along the bodywork, polished dark floor. Location shots: golden hour, blue hour or night with warm practical lights. Colour grade: deep greens, inky shadows, warm gold highlights, soft atmospheric mist, fine film grain, restrained saturation. Mood: calm, quiet, expensive. 2K, 16:9 unless stated. Blank number plates. No text, no lettering, no logos, no badges, no monograms, no watermark, no people's faces.

## Joining the journey clips for this site

5 clips x 8 s = 40 s. `js/config.js` expects 120 frames, so extract at 3 fps:

```bat
ffmpeg -i journey.mp4 -vf "fps=3,scale=1600:-2" -c:v libwebp -quality 70 frames\%03d.webp
```
