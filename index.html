Bronze and walnut, by hand

Every dial is turned from solid bronze and set into walnut cut from a single log, so the grain runs unbroken across the cabin.

Painted for you, coat by coat

Nine coats of lacquer, each rubbed back by hand. If your colour does not exist yet, our painters will mix it from a sample you bring.

Delivered by the person who built it

Your build lead hands over the key, rides with you for the first hour and stays on your phone for the first year.

From first conversation to first drive

Every Halcyon is commissioned, never ordered from a list. Nine months, four meetings, and one person who sees it through from start to finish.

  1. The conversation

    Week one

    We meet where you choose: the atelier, your home or your office. We talk about how you will use the car, not about options.

  2. The specification

    Weeks two to six

    Paint mixed to your sample, leather chosen from the hide, walnut matched from a single log. You sign off every piece by hand.

  3. The build

    Months three to nine

    Your build lead sends photographs at every stage and invites you to the atelier to see the body before it is painted.

  4. The handover

    The day

    The person who built your car hands you the key, rides with you for the first hour and stays on your phone for the first year.
